The difference

Most chicken is raised in
a building. Ours isn't.

01

Open pasture, always.

FlyFree birds spend their lives on grass, not concrete. Each bird has at minimum four square metres of real outdoor space. Year-round. No exceptions.

02

BC farms, named and verified.

Every bird is traceable to a specific farm in the Fraser Valley, Okanagan, or Vancouver Island. You know the land before you know the price.

03

No shortcuts on feed or welfare.

Non-GMO grain, no growth hormones, no antibiotics ever. Third-party welfare audited annually. The standards are simple because the supply chain is short.

Where it comes from

Fields wide enough that a chicken forgets it isn't wild.

The Fraser Valley holds some of the most productive agricultural land in Canada. FlyFree partners exclusively with multi-generational BC farm families who have committed to regenerative land practices. The birds move to fresh ground weekly, the land recovers, and the cycle continues.

Every delivery includes a provenance card naming the farm, the flock date, and the farmer. Not as a marketing touch. Because the member asked, and deserved an answer.
